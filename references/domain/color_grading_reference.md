# Color Grading Reference — Domain Reference

## Color Psychology & Mood

### Warm Colors (Red, Orange, Yellow)

| Color | Emotional Association | Typical Use | Caution |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|---------|
| **Red** | Passion, danger, urgency, anger | Intense moments, warnings, highlights | Can be overwhelming, attracts too much attention |
| **Orange** | Energy, warmth, enthusiasm | Golden hour, nostalgic warmth | Can appear cheap if oversaturated |
| **Yellow** | Happiness, optimism, attention | Bright environments, highlights | Difficult on skin tones, can appear sickly |

### Cool Colors (Blue, Green, Teal)

| Color | Emotional Association | Typical Use | Caution |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|---------|
| **Blue** | Calm, cold, isolation, technology | Night scenes, sci-fi, melancholy | Can feel distant or unemotional |
| **Green** | Nature, growth, sickness | Environments, horror (sickly) | Difficult on skin tones |
| **Teal/Cyan** | Futuristic, clinical, cinematic | Tech, sci-fi, teal-orange pairing | Can feel unnatural if overused |

### Neutrals (Black, White, Gray)

| Color | Emotional Association | Typical Use |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Black** | Mystery, death, sophistication | Noir, shadows, negative space |
| **White** | Purity, sterility, emptiness | Clean environments, highlights |
| **Gray** | Neutrality, ambiguity, industrial | Urban settings, uncertainty |

## Color Harmonies for Grading

### Complementary (Opposite on Color Wheel)

**Examples:**
- **Teal-Orange:** Most common cinematic pairing
  - Orange: Skin tones, warmth
  - Teal: Shadows, shadows, background
  - Effect: High contrast, subject separation, "cinematic" feel
- **Red-Green:** Christmas, festive, or complementary in landscapes
- **Blue-Orange:** Day/night contrast, warm highlights against cool shadows

### Analogous (Adjacent on Color Wheel)

**Examples:**
- **Warm Analogous:** Red-Orange-Yellow (sunsets, fire)
- **Cool Analogous:** Blue-Teal-Green (underwater, night)
- **Effect:** Harmonious, unified, low tension

### Triadic (Three Equally Spaced Colors)

**Examples:**
- **RGB Triad:** Red, Green, Blue (maximal color saturation)
- **Autumn Triad:** Orange, Red-Violet, Yellow (fall foliage)
- **Effect:** Balanced but vibrant, can be busy

### Monochromatic (Single Hue, Variations in Value/Saturation)

**Examples:**
- **Sepia:** Brown variations (nostalgia, old photographs)
- **Blue Monochrome:** Night scenes, isolation
- **Effect:** Unified, focused, emphasizes tonal range

## Common Color Grade Looks

### Bleach Bypass

**Characteristics:**
- Reduced saturation
- Increased contrast
- Silvery, metallic look
- Often used for action, war films

**In-game approximation:**
- Lower saturation to 30-50%
- Increase contrast
- Add slight blue tint to shadows

### Day for Night

**Characteristics:**
- Blue tint
- Reduced brightness
- Dark shadows
- Moonlit appearance

**In-game approximation:**
- Set time of day to night
- Add blue color grade (cool shadows)
- Lower exposure slightly
- Increase contrast

### Golden Hour

**Characteristics:**
- Warm highlights (orange/yellow)
- Soft shadows
- Low contrast
- Backlit glow

**In-game approximation:**
- Set time of day to sunrise/sunset
- Increase warmth (orange tint)
- Lower contrast
- Add glow/bloom effect

### High Contrast (Blockbuster)

**Characteristics:**
- Deep blacks
- Bright highlights
- Saturated colors
- Teal-orange shadows/highlights

**In-game approximation:**
- Maximize contrast
- Crush blacks slightly
- Saturation boost
- Add teal to shadows, orange to highlights

### Low Key (Noir)

**Characteristics:**
- Predominantly dark
- High contrast
- Deep shadows
- Selective highlights

**In-game approximation:**
- Lower exposure significantly
- Increase contrast
- Crush blacks
- Spotlight effect on key elements

### Monochrome / B&W

**Characteristics:**
- No color (or minimal color accent)
- Emphasis on tonality, texture, form
- Classic photography feel

**In-game approximation:**
- Desaturate completely
- Adjust contrast for desired look
- Optional: Add single color accent (red dress in B&W)

### Split Tone

**Characteristics:**
- Different colors for shadows vs highlights
- Example: Blue shadows, orange highlights
- Creates mood, separation

**In-game approximation:**
- Color grade shadows separately from highlights
- Common: Cool shadows (teal/blue), warm highlights (orange/gold)

### Vintage / Film Stock

**Characteristics:**
- Reduced saturation (muted colors)
- Slight color cast (yellow, magenta)
- Grain/noise
- Fade (lower contrast)

**In-game approximation:**
- Lower saturation (70-80%)
- Add warm tint
- Add grain effect
- Reduce contrast slightly

## Color Grade Settings by Mood

| Mood | Shadows | Midtones | Highlights | Saturation | Notes |
|------|---------|----------|------------|------------|-------|
| **Hopeful/optimistic** | Neutral | Warm | Bright warm | Normal-high | Increase warmth, maintain contrast |
| **Tense/threatening** | Cool | Neutral | Neutral-warm | Normal | Cool shadows, slight contrast boost |
| **Sad/melancholic** | Cool | Desaturated | Neutral | Low | Blue tint, lower saturation |
| **Nostalgic** | Warm | Warm | Warm | Reduced | Sepia or warm tint, lower contrast |
| **Mysterious** | Cool-dark | Neutral | Neutral | Normal | Dark shadows, cool color cast |
| **Energetic/action** | Deep | Normal | Bright | High | High contrast, saturated colors |
| **Calm/peaceful** | Neutral | Warm | Bright | Normal | Soft contrast, gentle warmth |
| **Horror/unsettling** | Green-tinted | Desaturated | Harsh | Low-mid | Sickly green, high contrast |

## Teal-Orange Grading (Cinematic Standard)

### Why It Works

1. **Skin tones** naturally fall in the orange range
2. **Teal** provides complementary contrast
3. **Separates** subject (skin/warm) from background (shadows/cool)
4. **High contrast** while maintaining color richness

### Application

1. **Identify skin tones:** Typically orange-red range
2. **Push shadows toward teal:** Blue-green bias
3. **Warm highlights:** Orange-yellow bias
4. **Maintain midtone neutrality:** Don't color entire frame
5. **Adjust intensity:** Subtle is often better than extreme

### Common Issues

- **Over-saturation:** Colors become cartoonish
- **Skin tones:** Can turn orange or green if too aggressive
- **Loss of nuance:** Other color relationships are lost
- **Cliché:** Very common in modern cinema

## Color Grade Workflow

### 1. Color Correction (Technical)

**Goal:** Neutral, accurate representation
- Balance white (correct color temperature)
- Balance exposure (correct brightness)
- Balance contrast (correct tonal range)
- Fix color casts (make greys neutral)

### 2. Color Grading (Creative)

**Goal:** Establish mood, style, narrative
- Apply overall look (warm/cool, high/low contrast)
- Split tone shadows/highlights
- Adjust saturation (global or selective)
- Add specific color biases for narrative effect

### 3. Final Adjustment

- Ensure skin tones remain natural
- Check for color clipping (loss of detail)
- Verify on different displays (color grading monitors vary)
- Consider final viewing environment (theater vs. mobile)

## Tools & Terminology

| Term | Definition | Application |
|------|------------|-------------|
| **Saturation** | Intensity of color | Low = muted, High = vivid |
| **Vibrance** | Saturation of less-saturated colors only | More natural than global saturation |
| **Hue** | Position on color wheel | Shift colors (red→orange) |
| **Luminance** | Brightness of color | Adjust brightness without changing color |
| **Lift** | Shadow brightness | Darken or lighten shadows |
| **Gamma** | Midtone brightness | Adjust middle tones |
| **Gain** | Highlight brightness | Adjust highlights |
| **Color Wheels** | Three wheels (Lift, Gamma, Gain) | Color grade by tonal range |
| **Curves** | Graphical brightness/color adjustment | Precise tonal control |

## In-Game Photo Mode Color Controls

### Common Controls

| Control | Function | Typical Range |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **Saturation** | Global color intensity | 0-200% (100 = normal) |
| **Contrast** | Difference between light/dark | 0-200% (100 = normal) |
| **Brightness** | Overall exposure | ±2 stops |
| **Color Temperature** | Warmth/coolness | Warm (yellow) ↔ Cool (blue) |
| **Tint** | Green-magenta shift | Green ↔ Magenta |
| **Vibrance** | Smart saturation | 0-100% |
| **Color Filters** | Apply specific color cast | Various presets |
| **Split Tone** | Different colors for shadows/highlights | Some advanced modes |

### Workflow

1. **Start with defaults:** Normal saturation, contrast
2. **Set overall mood:** Color temperature (warm/cool)
3. **Adjust intensity:** Saturation for desired effect
4. **Fine-tune:** Contrast, brightness, tint
5. **Advanced:** Split toning for shadow/highlight color
6. **Test:** Compare to original, ensure skin tones natural
